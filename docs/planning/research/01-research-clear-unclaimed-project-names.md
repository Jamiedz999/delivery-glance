# Research clear, unclaimed project names

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Which direct English names made from two familiar words clearly convey recipient-facing Delivery visibility or arrival, without implying fleet management, route optimisation, or broad logistics, and have no exact conflicting software project or product discoverable through GitHub, general web search, relevant package registries, or obvious trademark/product checks? Produce a ranked shortlist with evidence, clarity trade-offs, and collision risks for a human naming decision.

## Answer

Six candidates passed the bounded exact-collision screen. **Delivery Glance**, **Delivery Ahead**, and **Delivery Peek** offer the strongest clarity/availability trade-off; **Delivery Beacon**, **Delivery Nearby**, and **Delivery Snapshot** remain lower-ranked alternatives with semantic caveats. No final name was selected. See [Clear, unclaimed project-name research](../research/clear-unclaimed-project-names.md) for the evidence, exclusions, limitations, and decision guidance.
